HumanOS TF-IDF/logistic-regression baseline trained using Google Research GoEmotions.
Dataset: https://github.com/google-research/google-research/tree/master/goemotions
Paper: Demszky et al., GoEmotions: A Dataset of Fine-Grained Emotions (ACL 2020).
Reddit-origin text is not bundled in this artifact. Model weights are not a psychological assessment.
